## Career Goals
My interest in data science developed through my personal interest in political data and analysis. That interest initially grew from listening to the FiveThirtyEight podcast, which introduced me to the ways statistical analysis, polling, and data modeling could be used to better understand elections and political trends. Over time, that curiosity continued through podcasts like GD Politics and Still Counting, which further developed my interest in the relationship between data, public opinion, and real-world consequences.

My career goal is to pursue a position in data analysis or data science where I can apply statistical methods and technical skills to analyze political, social, or other real-world data. I am particularly interested in working with datasets involving public policy, elections, demographics, and social trends. I find it interesting how data can reveal patterns that might not otherwise be obvious and provide additional context for complicated issues. Ideally, I would like to work in an environment where I can use quantitative analysis to help organizations better understand information and make informed decisions.

To work toward this goal, I am continuing to develop my technical skills in Python, SQL, R, and database management while building a stronger foundation in statistics and mathematics. Through my coursework at Oregon State University, I have begun developing the programming and analytical skills necessary to work with larger datasets and draw meaningful conclusions from them. My previous exploration of software engineering also helped me recognize that I am more interested in the analytical and mathematical side of computing than software development itself.

Although I am still exploring the different career opportunities within data science, I hope to combine my technical education and personal interest in political and social data into a career that allows me to investigate complex problems and use data to better understand the world.
